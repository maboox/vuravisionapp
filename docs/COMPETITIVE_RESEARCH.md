# Competitive research

Current product research confirms several interaction patterns worth keeping in VuraVision without copying proprietary assets.

## BenQ EZWrite
Current EZWrite emphasizes a scalable canvas, scroll mode, split-screen/browser workflows, floating annotation across other apps, screenshot-to-whiteboard, handwriting recognition, math conversion/calculation, geometry tools and palm-style erasing. VuraVision should keep whiteboard-first launch and later add an officially-supported overlay/capture flow.

Source: https://www.benq.eu/en-eu/education/software/ezwrite-6-whiteboard.html

## ViewSonic myViewBoard 3.0
myViewBoard 3.0 adds a redesigned consistent toolbar, navigation map for a large canvas, long-press access, file management, export preview and QR sharing. It supports infinite canvas/page management and imports media as canvas content. Its QR flow is cloud-oriented; VuraVision deliberately differentiates with local-LAN QR sharing that does not require internet.

Sources:
- https://support.viewsonic.com/en/support/solutions/articles/33000315934-what-is-the-difference-between-the-myviewboard-2-0-and-myviewboard-3-0-toolbar-
- https://myviewboard.com/kb/insert-external-objects/mvb30-file-management
- https://myviewboard.com/kb/en_US/whiteboard-working-area/wb-features-comparison1

## Design implications for VuraVision
1. Keep startup directly in Whiteboard.
2. Preserve canvas area with a compact top toolbar and secondary menu.
3. Treat PDF/image/media as objects, not separate apps.
4. Keep QR sharing offline/LAN-first.
5. Keep Engineering/touch calibration as a differentiator for 65–115 inch panels.
6. Prioritize consistent cross-module design before expanding the number of Lab/Games items.
